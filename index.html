Our Story

About Sakthi Dental Clinic

Get to Know Dr. Anupriya

Your Trusted Partner in Dental Care

Bringing over 20 years of expertise, Dr. Anupriya stands as a leading figure in modern dentistry at Hosur. Graduating in 2000, she refined her clinical skills during six impactful years at Mathura Clinic.

In 2004, Dr. Anupriya established Sakthi Dental Clinic. Her dedication is also reflected in her long-standing service as a trusted dental consultant at the Primary Health Center at Chandara Hospital for over a decade.

Our Mission

To redefine oral healthcare by delivering personalized, compassionate, and advanced dental services. We integrate state-of-the-art technology with patient-centric care to ensure every treatment enhances your smile and well-being.

Our Vision

To be a leading force in modern dentistry, known for setting new standards in patient care, innovation, and community engagement. We aspire to deliver exceptional dental outcomes and contribute positively to the community.

A-Z Care

Complete Dental Treatments

From preventive care to advanced surgical procedures, we provide comprehensive solutions to keep your smile healthy and radiant.

Teeth Cleaning & Scaling

Advanced, non-surgical gum care using modern LASER technology. Scaling safely removes plaque and tartar.

Tooth Filling

Expert tooth filling services using high-quality materials to repair cavities and preserve natural structure.

Tooth Extraction

Careful evaluation and gentle extraction techniques to minimize discomfort in a stress-free environment.

Complete Denture

High-quality complete dentures to restore function and aesthetics, providing a comfortable fit for missing teeth.

Dental Implants

Reliable solutions using biocompatible titanium artificial roots to restore appearance and structural strength.

Laser Dentistry

Minimally invasive soft tissue surgeries and gum care offering faster healing and reduced discomfort.

Root Canal Therapy

Careful removal of infected tissue to relieve pain and preserve your natural tooth for long-term health.

Wisdom Tooth Extraction

Gentle removal of impacted or problematic wisdom teeth using modern anesthesia for a pain-free experience.

Fixed Bridge

Custom-made dental bridges anchored to adjacent teeth or implants to improve chewing function.

Teeth Whitening

Professional services using safe agents to lighten teeth by several shades, treating deep stains.

Veneers

Thin, custom-made shells designed to cover imperfections like chips or discoloration for a flawless smile.

Pediatric Dentistry

Gentle, comprehensive care for children in a welcoming environment, building healthy lifetime habits.

Flap Surgery

For advanced gum disease, lifting gum tissue to remove deep-seated bacteria, supporting optimal healing.

Orthodontic Braces

Correct misaligned teeth with metal, ceramic, or lingual braces, or transparent clear aligners.
