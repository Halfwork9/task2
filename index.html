About Us

Get to Know Dr. Anupriya

Your Trusted Partner in Dental Care

Bringing over 20 years of expertise, Dr. Anupriya stands as a leading figure in modern dentistry at Hosur. She began her professional journey after graduating in 2000. Her passion for delivering exceptional dental care was further nurtured during six impactful years at Mathura Clinic, where she refined her clinical skills and deepened her commitment to patient wellbeing.

In 2004, Dr. Anupriya established Sakthi Dental Clinic in Hosur with a clear vision to make high-quality dental care accessible to all. Her dedication extends beyond private practice, reflected in her long-standing service with the Primary Health Center at Chandara Hospital, where she has been a trusted dental consultant for over a decade.

At Sakthi Dental Clinic, we believe that a healthy smile is a gateway to confidence and wellbeing. Our clinic blends advanced dental technology with a warm, patient-friendly environment to ensure every visit is comfortable and stress-free. Whether you're looking for preventive care, cosmetic enhancements, or restorative solutions, Dr. Anupriya and her team are committed to delivering excellence at every step. Because here, your smile isn’t just treated—it’s celebrated.

Comprehensive Dental Treatments

From preventive care to advanced surgical procedures, we provide comprehensive solutions to keep your smile healthy and radiant.

Teeth Cleaning & Scaling

Advanced, non-surgical gum care using modern LASER technology. Scaling removes plaque and tartar, serving as a crucial preparatory step before any surgical intervention.

Tooth Filling

Expert tooth filling services using high-quality materials to repair damage caused by cavities, trauma, or wear, preserving your natural tooth structure.

Tooth Extraction

When necessary, we perform extractions using gentle techniques to minimize discomfort in a stress-free environment, making it as smooth and painless as possible.

Artificial Complete Denture

High-quality, precision-crafted complete dentures to restore function and aesthetics, providing a natural look and comfortable fit for missing teeth.

Dental Implants

A modern, reliable solution using biocompatible titanium artificial roots to securely place prosthetic teeth, restoring both appearance and strength.

Laser Dentistry

Advanced, minimally invasive treatments for soft tissue surgeries and gum care, offering faster healing, reduced discomfort, and enhanced accuracy.

Root Canal Therapy

Careful removal of infected tissue to relieve pain, eliminate infection, and preserve your natural tooth structure when infection reaches the pulp.

Wisdom Tooth Extraction

Gentle and effective removal of impacted or problematic wisdom teeth using modern techniques and anesthesia for a pain-free experience.

Fixed Partial Denture (Bridge)

Custom-made dental bridges anchored to adjacent teeth or implants to restore your smile's appearance and improve chewing function.

Teeth Whitening (Bleaching)

Professional services using safe agents to lighten teeth by several shades, treating both external and internal stains to restore natural radiance.

Veneers

Thin, custom-made shells designed to cover imperfections like chips, gaps, or discoloration, providing a flawless and natural-looking smile.

Pediatric Dentistry

Gentle, comprehensive care for children in a welcoming environment, focusing on building healthy dental habits for a lifetime of bright smiles.

Flap Surgery

For advanced gum disease, lifting the gum tissue to remove deep-seated plaque and bacteria, supporting optimal healing and preventing periodontal issues.

Orthodontic Braces & Aligners

Correct misaligned teeth with metal, ceramic, or lingual braces, or choose discreet, removable clear aligners for comfort and flexibility.
